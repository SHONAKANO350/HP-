とらいについて

とらいは、千葉県我孫子市東我孫子の住宅街にたたずむ、和食・魚料理のお店です。
焼き魚やお造りに、ご飯・味噌汁・小鉢を添えた定食スタイルで、日々の食事をお楽しみいただけます。
紺色の暖簾と鉢植えが出迎える玄関先も、とらいらしい佇まいのひとつです。
お店の雰囲気は、こだわりのページでさらに詳しくご紹介しています。

【】 ① First visit to a restaurant I'd been curious about. The Japanese atmosphere was very calming. I ordered the fried horse mackerel set meal from the lunch menu. The owner was running the place by himself and seemed busy, but it arrived after a short wait. The fried horse mackerel was large. The taste was good, and the preparation must have been good because I didn't notice any small bones. There was more than enough tartar sauce, and I enjoyed it to the very last bite. The side dishes of kinpira, pickles, and miso soup were also delicious. I should have asked how they recommended eating the soft-boiled egg. I will visit again. Thank you for the meal.
