トラットリア キタッラについて

トラットリア キタッラは、千葉県我孫子市寿にあるイタリア料理店です。
クリーム仕立てやトマトソース仕立て、バジルの香るジェノベーゼ仕立てなど、彩り豊かなパスタを中心にお料理をご用意しています。
木の温もりが感じられる店内で、気取らずお食事の時間をお過ごしいただけます。
お店の雰囲気は、こだわりのページでさらに詳しくご紹介しています。

お客様の声

Googleに投稿された口コミのうち、Google側が選定した最大5件を、原文のまま掲載しています。内容は投稿者ご本人の感想であり、当店が作成した情報ではありません。

  • ★★★★☆ 4か月前

    This is a privately owned Italian restaurant. It used to be an Italian restaurant before, but the management has changed. The relaxed atmosphere makes dining enjoyable. The salad, soup, and pasta are generous portions and will leave you completely full. Ordering the large portion of pasta means a bigger bowl, which is well worth the extra 300 yen! If you take the bus from Abiko Station, you can even enjoy some wine. I'll definitely take the bus next time! 🍷

    mixunさん(Googleクチコミ)

  • ★★★★★ 7か月前

    Saturday lunch We arrived just after 1:00 PM, and a seat had just opened up. Parking was difficult to find, so we used a nearby coin parking lot. The lunch was absolutely delicious! Lunch salads are often served on small plates, but this one was served on a standard 26cm plate, as you can see in the photo. This was a nice touch. And it was delicious!

    hisae kawaiさん(Googleクチコミ)

  • ★★★★☆ 11か月前

    I went for lunch. I ordered a set meal, which included a choice of cold soup or salad and a drink, all for around 2000 yen. The price was reasonable, and the food was delicious. The ricotta cheese and eggplant pasta (Narma style) was incredibly tasty; it was a restaurant-quality dish that I couldn't replicate at home. The large pieces of eggplant were tender, and the melted ricotta cheese mixed with the hot pasta was exquisite. The lunch pasta menu seems to change monthly, so I'd like to try different kinds. I'd also like to visit for dinner!

    SKI YKさん(Googleクチコミ)

  • ★★★★★ 1年前

    I went there after seeing the reviews. The restaurant has a casual atmosphere with a visible kitchen. It feels easy to drop in. I ordered the daily special salad, the bonito and onion salad, and the carbonara and pancetta tomato sauce pasta. The daily special salad used melon, making it refreshing, and the contrast in texture between the melon and the leafy greens was superb. The bonito was excellent, with the flavor of the ingredient brought out while the fishy smell was perfectly eliminated. The carbonara, in particular, deserves special mention; the aroma the moment it entered my mouth was second to none compared to any carbonara I've ever had. I feel like I've been missing out by not knowing about this restaurant so close by. It was delicious. I will definitely visit again.

    Fletcher hさん(Googleクチコミ)

  • ★★☆☆☆ 6か月前

    It was mushy and not tasty. It tasted like something an amateur had made at home and had failed. I regret ordering a large portion. The pesto was supposed to be made with homemade ricotta cheese, but the balance was delicate and it didn't go well. The nuts, which were supposed to be delicious, didn't have any positive effect. I had high hopes after reading the reviews, but maybe I was just disappointed.
