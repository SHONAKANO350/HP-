ARUKASについて

我孫子の街なかにたたずむARUKASは、木のカウンターを思わせる落ち着いた空間で、ワインやクラフトビールとともに洋風の一皿を楽しめるような雰囲気のお店です。仕事帰りにひとりで、あるいは気の置けない相手とふらりと立ち寄れる、そんな時間を過ごしていただけます。Googleでは4.3の評価(クチコミ46件)をいただいております。

★★★★★ 7か月前

I am from the US and have been to Arukas a few times with my wife and have always been very impressed. The food is amazing and served as you go. Have a drink, order a dish and enjoy conversation with a friend or even the owner himself. The owner does it all; he is the waiter, bartender and chef all at once. The slim location can hold maybe 20-25 people and most are comfortable right at the bar seeing all the menu items prepared fresh right in front of your eyes. Food pacing is not slow but you will require patience as some dishes take longer than others. The menu is always changing based on locally curated foods but a few staples are the Horse Carpaccio and pâté. I wish I had more pictures of all the tasty dishes I tried but they don’t last long once they get placed in front of you. A great wine selection as well as any liquors and a few beers on tap. If you are ever in Abiko please find Arukas. Just a few minutes walk from JR line Abiko station.

投稿者: Jason Snodgrassさん(Googleマップ)

★★★★★ 3年前

Omiyuki Shokudo pops up at Alukas only on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday afternoons 😊 The menu changes every week, and everything looks so delicious that I always have a hard time deciding! And everything I've tried has been truly delicious! For 990 yen, you get a main dish and side dishes, plus salad, rice, miso soup, and pickles or dessert – it's amazing! (Some main dishes may incur an additional charge.) There's also a "quick drink set" for 1100 yen, which includes three side dishes and a lunch alcoholic beverage. Anyway, Miyuki's food is so delicious that I wish they were open every day, not just Friday, Saturday, and Sunday (lol). I also really love Alukas at night ♡ Their homemade sangria is so good I order a carafe! 😂
