★★★★★

Last Saturday, three friends and I visited the restaurant just after 2:00 PM. Since it was late, we were the only other customer there, and we were able to find a table right away. The interior had a classic restaurant feel, and it reminded me of when my parents brought me here as a kid, but it was actually my first time here (lol). It's a restaurant that evokes nostalgia. Now, let's order. My two friends ordered the daily lunch special, which included an Italian hamburger steak. I ordered the stew omelet rice (a crazy combination of two of my favorite dishes! 🤤). After chatting for a while, our lunch menus arrived. The visuals were mouthwatering! 😙 My stew omelet rice was brought out shortly after. It was also mouthwatering! 😊 First, a bite. The flavor was so nostalgic, I couldn't help but wonder if I'd ever been here before. 🤔 (It really was my first time, though. 😚) Wow, it's so delicious. I'll definitely be back! 😊 My two friends also raved about the Italian hamburger. We finished off by enjoying a relaxing time over coffee. It's such a cozy place. When paying, I promised the owner to come back before leaving. I'd love to come back again soon! 🙂 Next time, I'll probably try the Italian hamburger. ☺️

It's been an incredibly long time since I've had curry served in this type of bowl. I didn't know until it arrived, so my heart fluttered when it was served. The katsu curry—the rice, the curry sauce, the katsu—perfectly fulfilled everything I expect from katsu curry served at a coffee shop. My wife said her daily special was also delicious. The staff were friendly, and I really liked the retro feel of the shop. Since I occasionally have errands in that area, I've decided this will be my go-to place for lunch from now on.
