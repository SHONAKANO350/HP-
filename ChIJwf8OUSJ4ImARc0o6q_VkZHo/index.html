★★★★★ 10か月前

We went there for lunch. The appetizers were very generous in portion size, and we shared the pasta between the two of us. The main course, a meat dish, was also just the right amount for two people! It was delicious and reasonably priced – we were very satisfied. We'll definitely come back when we visit the Shonan Roadside Station again.

投稿者: 池野忠義

★★★★☆ 1年前

I made a reservation for lunch. Lunch options are A (2,200 yen) and B (2,800 yen). I chose B, which comes with a main course. Both A and B offer two pasta options (see photo). The appetizer salad plate was generous and satisfying. The namayatsumu was also delicious. The fuoccacia was warm and delicious, so I asked for a refill, but unfortunately, they didn't have any. The pasta portion was very small. The main fish had a crispy crust, and the accompanying vegetables were tasty. The meat also looked delicious. The dessert was panna cotta (a simple, familiar dish). Coffee was also included in the set. The restaurant was only accepting reservations that day, and it was fully booked. The seats with a view were fully booked with a group. The seats overlooking Lake Teganuma through the large windows offer a spectacular view, so I would definitely request one next time.

投稿者: MIKA YAMAMOTO

★★★★☆ 3年前

This popular restaurant offers delicious food and a view of Lake Teganuma, so reservations are recommended. The lunch set menu comes in two options: A (¥1900) or B (¥2400). The difference is whether or not a fish or meat dish is included; the salad, pasta, drink, dessert, and fucaccia are the same. The salad was piled high with fresh vegetables, and the accompanying salami sausage was especially delicious. While the food takes a while to arrive, the pasta, sea bream sauté, and panna cotta dessert were beautifully and delicately presented, reflecting the chef's dedication. Reservations are difficult to obtain, but I would definitely like to visit again.

投稿者: mr. buono

★★★★★ 4か月前
